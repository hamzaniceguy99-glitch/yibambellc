Program 02 · Honest about the length

Full groom

We agree a length at check-in after looking at the actual coat, not the photograph. If it is matted underneath, the result will be shorter than you hoped, and clipping can reveal sores that the mat was hiding. We would rather show you that at the start.

  • 2 to 3 hours
  • Dogs and cats
  • Per visit
  • Realistic expectations

Program 03 · Not shaving a double coat

De-shedding

De-shedding removes loose undercoat and makes a real difference to the house. Shaving a double-coated breed does not, and can permanently change how the coat regrows — so we will decline that and explain why, even in a Florida August.

  • 1 to 2 hours
  • Heavy-coated dogs
  • Per visit
  • Patience from the dog
